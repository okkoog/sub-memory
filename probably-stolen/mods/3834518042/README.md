# Better Merchants

Ever wonder why the retired traders never actually retire? In Better Merchants, retired traders, suppliers, and some new faces sell their goods after hours at 10 new shops alongside Dr. Jackson.

### New Night Shops:
- Retired traders stop visiting your store and sell from their own shops, after one thank-you visit with a discounted first batch
- Suppliers you could only reach by phone open night shops with the Night Shop upgrade; Shareholder adds a discount there
- A couple of shadier dealers set up shop too, once you know where to look
- A setting brings back the old store visits

### Merchants:
- Greenhouse, Arms & Ammo, Water Shop and Gutter Market
- Red Imp Brewery, Underground Chemist, Ashvin Clinic and GP Mining
- Unmarked Stall and Hypertech Den

### How the shops work:
- Fire sales, shop specials, troubles, price hikes, thefts and closing days keep you shopping around
- Stock shows everything before duplicates and favours what you use most
- Other mods' goods sell at the shops that fit them
- A setting lets Dr. Jackson keep selling his full stock
- Prices follow your difficulty's markups

All settings are under the gear button in the Mods menu.

### For mod devs:
Other mods can sell through the shops with a loot table named better_merchants.stock.<slot>.<modId>

Items other mods add are also auto-stocked by item type. An item with no mapped type that carries the game's fish, roe, fry or animal tag sells at the Gutter Market, where the vanilla aquafarmer's and rancher's goods sell, at the vanilla price for its kind when its value is 0. A mod that registers any better_merchants.stock table is curated: none of its items is auto-stocked, so its own tables and upgrade gates decide what sells. To opt out, register a table; an item in any of the shops' tables is never auto-stocked either. The same goes for goods a mod adds to Dr. Jackson's counter: a curated mod's stay with him, and so do those of a mod whose toggle is off.

Items worth 200 or more can also turn up at the Unmarked Stall, at most one a night and none worth over 5 times the dearest vanilla one there; the same opt-out applies.
