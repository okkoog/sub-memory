# Better Merchants

Buy from your suppliers and retired traders in person: their goods move to new night shops on the transit screen, with nothing in the vanilla game removed.

### Supplier shops:
- A new Night Shop Wild upgrade opens a shop for each supplier line: Red Imp Brewery, Chem Supply, Pharmacy and GP Mining
- Each shop sells the line's phone goods and more besides, at most one of any machine
- Shareholder now needs the Night Shop upgrade, and adds a discount at that shop
- A setting opens each night shop with the supplier's first upgrade instead, with no Night Shop upgrade

### Retired traders:
- Retirement upgrades become investments: the trader visits once with a discounted first batch, then sells from a shop
- The farmer, rancher and aquafarmer sell at the Greenhouse; the gunsmith and water trader get the Gun Shop and Water Shop

### Unmarked Stall:
- A new Informant upgrade at Wild leads to a stall under a staircase, where a thief sells from his stash
- Open on Friday and Saturday nights only, with a new stash each night
- Sells rare finds at twice their value: pristine cassettes you are missing, sealed supply crates, keycards, and more!

### Other changes:
- Now and then a theft thins a shop's stock for a day, and each shop closes one day a week
- Shops also stock other mods' items that fit them, a different pick each visit, with settings for how much and which mods
- Prices carry your difficulty's markups, as the game's own sellers charge them, with a setting for base prices

A shop visit is a normal trip.

It adds a lot of shops, so with many destination mods installed, [Unified Transit Hub](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3836809715) keeps the transit screen easy to get around.

All settings are under the gear button in the Mods menu.

### What's new in 1.2.5:
- Night shops follow the difficulty's markups
- New night shop: the Unmarked Stall

### For mod devs:
Other mods can sell through the shops with a loot table named better_merchants.stock.<slot>.<modId>

Items other mods add are also auto-stocked by item type. A mod that registers any better_merchants.stock table is curated: none of its items is auto-stocked, so its own tables and upgrade gates decide what sells. To opt out, register a table; an item in any of the shops' tables is never auto-stocked either.

Items worth 200 or more can also turn up at the Unmarked Stall, at most one a night and none worth over five times the dearest vanilla one there; the same opt-out applies.
