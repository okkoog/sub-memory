# Extended Appraisals

A standalone Probably Stolen mod for 0.46D. Rat Vandelay needs an appraiser who can price a sealed shipment without opening it. He settles at the end of the week. His runners never tell you whether your quote was right.

**Play:** Extract the ExtendedAppraisals folder under the game’s Mods folder and enable Extended Appraisals in the mod menu. To meet Vandelay, finish one plasma appraisal visit with at least three jars and quote every jar exactly on your first attempt. He visits when you open the shop the following day. You can decline at either stage of the offer. Accept and he gives you a contraband Rx field guide and his business card. Use the magnifier on a runner’s bottle, check its manufacturer logo and foil against the registered specimen, then submit a quote using the normal appraisal controls. The opening quote is 500 credits.

**The books:** Runners visit on days 2, 4 and 6 of each seven-day cycle. A runner carries two, three or four bottles (20% / 60% / 20%). Quote each separately. Each inspected bottle earns a fifth of its resale price, minus the difference between that price and your quote in either direction. For example, a 500-credit bottle quoted at 500 earns 100; a 200-credit bottle quoted at 500 loses 260. Rat leaves a level-two contraband evaluation on your side of the counter at closing. Open the 2x2 sheet at handoff for the illustrated bottle breakdown. He forgives the first negative appraisal week. For a balance owed, call him within the next two nights and leave the requested goods in the trash that night. He credits 85% of their value. Promotional exchanges can go through when the remaining goods cover the debt.

**The guide:** Five fictional brands use different caps, manufacturer logos and foil positions. Each accepts one foil mark, a star or a white circle. A missing or unrelated manufacturer logo exposes a counterfeit. The three registered direct parents earn an 85-credit premium when their logo appears. A shrink sleeve can carry its own marked foil stripe or serve as plain wrapping. A sole matching stripe covers the foil requirement. Rat pays for a second foil, and charges once for a wrong mark. Archive stock needs a whole sleeve and the direct parent logo, or the own logo of a brand without a parent. Inspectors may seize it even inside the dossier. Call 83719 to request a replacement. A courier brings it the next day and the next settlement includes a 250-credit deduction.

**Default:** Vandelay freezes the alarm and its contents, blocks insurance and tanks black-market reputation. During collection, Dr. Jackson withholds alarm modules. The thief leaves empty-handed on the first night, even if you own no alarm. On subsequent nights, a thief takes an eligible item when available. Rat counts 85% of the value lost. Once the total reaches twice the goods he originally demanded, Dr. Jackson visits the next morning. Accept to resume the contract. Decline and he takes the alarm; his shop can sell you a bare replacement. Ordinary faction restrictions can still affect insurance afterward.

**Build:** Run Build.ps1; the package is written to dist/ExtendedAppraisals. Pass -GameDir for another installation or -Install to copy it to that game's Mods directory. The dialogue, ledger and field guide support English, Spanish and Simplified Chinese. The runtime needs no Python or web service. The optional art rebuild uses Pillow and UnityPy. The guide and weekly evaluation enter the regular contraband inspection when kept in the dossier.

**Verify:** Run tools/Verify.ps1 after building for valuation, settlement, and scanner checks. Run tools/check_layers.py to check the printed logos and all bottle foil and shrink sleeve variants.



**Shipment mix:** New bottles roll 18% counterfeit, each with one house-specific or general fault. The five brands appear at roughly equal rates. Moth Bell, Vesper Loom and Cinder Finch each accept a different registered parent. Eligible houses carry their parent logo 33% of the time. Before any counterfeit substitutions, fills are 60% / 28% / 12% for 500 / 600 / 700 mL. Cophanta is 20%, cap leaflets 33%, expiry 20%, scuffs 20% and lifted foil 10%. Moth Bell and Vesper Loom use water-sensitive ink with a 15% chance of smudging. Cinder Finch, Frost Spindle and Ember Stag use waterproof ink. A smudge on any of those three is a counterfeit fault.

The 0.46D reference generators are PreBuiltItemHelper.CreateRibwichRandom (10% opened, then 15% counterfeit), CreateRandomInjector (20% expired, then 25% unusable, then 30% counterfeit), CreateHandRandom (10% premium, then 20% combat, then 25% junk), and CreatePlasmaRandom (6% lab, followed by conditional house rolls). These are sequential rolls. The syrup uses its own weights and replays older seeds and translates their issuer into a visible logo.
