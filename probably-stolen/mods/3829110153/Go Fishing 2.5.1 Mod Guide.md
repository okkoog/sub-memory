# Go Fishing 2.5.1 Mod Guide

Detailed parameters, the full fish species tables, and calculation examples are in [Go Fishing 2.5.1 Detailed Stats Guide](<Go Fishing 2.5.1 Detailed Stats Guide.md>).

Applies to the standalone "Go Fishing" 2.5.1. No ItemForge or standalone English patch is required. Chinese, English, Russian and French follow the game language. The current manifest lists Fishing Cuisine Expansion as a prerequisite.

## Quick Start

1. The in-shop boat key interaction is now always visible. It cannot be used until repairs are done, showing "the ship is under repair"; after completing the mentor's first payment you gain sailing eligibility. The repair debt totals 1000 credits; you can pay 100 first to unlock — no need to clear the full debt first.
2. After unlocking sailing, wait for Willie to visit. On first introduction he gives you the fishing terminal along with his business card; subsequent gear and intel unlock with the storyline.
3. Prepare a fishing rod, bait, and necessary supplies, and verify your carried status on the voyage prep page. Do short trips first to learn the map, resource consumption, and return methods.
4. After returning to port, process the formal fish hold and cargo crates; separate fish for research, farming, and processing.
5. Expand farming only after you have stable water, filtration, and feed. Start with a few fish to learn daily consumption; do not cram the farm full.
6. Choose whole-fish sale, filleting, drying, canning, or research by purpose; a single fish cannot be used in multiple of these routes simultaneously.

For your first voyage, aim for "come back safely and unload the cargo." Fish value depends on species, weight, grade, and processing method — no single processing route guarantees the highest profit for every fish.

## Willie & the Terminal

Choose the single fish king hand-in option. It takes one eligible fish from the desk first, then checks your inventory. Golden Fish, Blue Crystal Fish, Lofurin and Niorun are eligible.

### Visits & Unlocking

Willie does not appear the same day you see the key. After unlocking the ship, the first introduction is scheduled at earliest the next day. At least 4 days after the first introduction, the old-fishing-net visit can occur. After net repair, the main quest prioritizes bait experiments; advanced bait, rod research, and later king-fish intel advance in quest order.

| Stage | What to do | Common misconception |
|---|---|---|
| Mentor & ship | Complete first payment to gain sailing eligibility | Always-visible key ≠ first payment waived |
| Willie introduction | Receive business card and fishing terminal | Terminal is not held back until the farming system unlocks |
| Old net & bait | Read the visit quest and deliver required materials | Waiting days alone cannot skip delivery |
| Fish research | Complete unlocked research and follow-up visits | Different visits have wait intervals and prerequisites |
| King fish & planet intel | Advance the current quest, acquire advanced gear | Advanced fish are not all unlocked by just swapping a rod |

The above is process order; check quests and dialogue for the specific delivery lists, fees, and current progress. When multiple conditions are met, the main quest takes priority; no visit today is not necessarily a corrupted save.

### Terminal Purchases

Double-click the fishing terminal to open its interface; view codex, research, and shop via tabs. The shop shows categories by current unlock state; categories with no purchasable items may not appear. Willie's physical shelf purchase method is still retained.

Ordinary goods use plus/minus buttons to select quantity; limited-purchase items like farming equipment follow their own quotas — one per type per week, based on the interface's remaining quota and reset time. Do not treat one device's remaining quota as a count shared by all devices. Salt, chili, fish oil, super salt, and blazing chili are on the unlimited-purchase whitelist and can be restocked anytime.

Purchasing requires cash, unlock, and storage-space conditions. The system tries to place items into the main inventory or warehouse, deducting payment and registering the purchase limit only after successful delivery. If you can't buy, first make continuous space that fits the item's shape.

The terminal and various manuals are single-copy items: they are not sold repeatedly while owned, and can be re-purchased if lost or sold, one copy at a time. Old saves that have passed the gifting storyline have a reissue flow; check the warehouse and pending items first.

Willie's door-to-door purchase is still limited to existing fish, fish materials, food, and bait — not all of this mod's tools and equipment. It now reads items' original-vanilla mod source markers; goods clearly from other mods are not treated as this mod's goods just because of the same name. Fish offal, crab-sourced vanilla meat, and unmarked existing catalog items retain their own recognition paths.

## Voyage Guide

### Prep & Supplies

Before sailing, check ship status, power, available supplies, fishing gear, bait, and fish-hold space in order. The prep page's actual carried state is authoritative — having something in the shop warehouse does not mean it will be usable this voyage.

The rod is for fishing operations; nets and other tools are used per scene prompts. High-tier goals may be constrained by story, intel, and gear simultaneously. When preparing repair, resupply, and emergency materials, leave margin for the round trip; do not spend all power and supplies reaching the target.

### Time, Sea State & Map

The voyage map is 19×15 tiles; the voyage is timed from 18:00, and you should return by 04:00 the next day at latest, with a single cap of 600 game minutes. Only one formal voyage per night; closing the interface does not mean you've safely returned. The ordinary fish hold starts at 6×6 and can grow via ship upgrades. Ordinary salvage resources refresh on a 2-day cycle; fixed terrain, exploration state, and resource refresh are different records — refresh does not mean all terrain and loot reset at once.

Navigation, search, and other actions advance voyage time. Time is not a background number to ignore: natural disturbance does not grow in the first 240 minutes; after 240 minutes it gains a base 5 per 30 minutes, 8 after 360 minutes, and 12 after 480 minutes, further modified by sea state and other factors. A calm night multiplies natural growth by 0.7. Linguan has its own disturbance growth rules.

These numbers describe base growth, not a guaranteed safe countdown. Sea state, travel method, events, and ship status all change your actual situation. Reassess when you see risk prompts rather than waiting until a fixed minute to return.

| Map info | How to use |
|---|---|
| Discovered nodes | View currently reachable positions and connection routes |
| Fish spots | Act after checking available gear, bait, and target conditions |
| Hunt targets | Track using current position and movement clues, reserving resources for pursuit |
| Floating debris, wrecks, etc. | Choose search or salvage, bearing the corresponding time and risk |
| Ports | View current executable options: resupply, services, return |
| Hidden points | Satisfy key and entrance conditions first, then decide whether to enter |

Discovering a location does not mean you can cross obstacles to enter directly. A key-room mark on the map also does not mean the entrance has been cleared.

### Fishing & Tension

Enter a fish spot and choose a fishing method from current options. While fighting the fish after casting, watch both the fish's state and tension, adjusting with the reeling, relaxing, and other actions the interface provides. Chasing speed while constantly pushing tension into the danger zone loses controllable margin; being too conservative also consumes time.

Different fish, spots, and gear change choices and fight behavior — a "keep pressing the same button" universal solution does not apply. Get familiar with state changes using ordinary fish before challenging king fish. Before a fish is successfully collected, do not treat it as already loaded into the hold.

While dragging an item in the cargo hold, press Q or E, or right-click to rotate it.

### Hunt, Salvage & Return

Hunting requires keeping up with the target's actions and position changes; when planning routes, account for search, engagement, and return to port together. Salvage may likewise require multiple actions; search progress does not mean all materials are obtained.

The formal fish hold is this voyage's cargo space. Picked-up items take grid space and shape; when space is tight, reorganize first, then decide what to keep. Some special keys are not ordinary shop items: for example, the Blood Key and Life Key can be used in the hold for corresponding hidden points, entering the equipment library and being carried automatically after being brought back; each batch of hidden supplies consumes one key on first entry, and re-entry within the batch does not consume again. They cannot be sold or transferred to the shop backpack.

Before returning, confirm the actual operation conditions the port provides, and process crates, fish hold, and pending items after completing the return. Do not assume rewards are in shop inventory just because you saw a reward prompt at sea. If the distress beacon trigger condition is met, rescue automatically consumes a beacon and escorts you back, settling the hold as a normal return; the beacon is an emergency measure — still actively manage your return.

## Scavenging Fishing Trait

"Simple Scavenging Fishing" is a separate 2-point starting trait. After choosing it and carrying suitable gear, you can attempt fishing in the corresponding scavenging flow.

| Item | Simple Scavenging Fishing | Formal voyage |
|---|---|---|
| Entry | Starting trait & scavenging flow | Ship unlock, prep & sailing |
| Catch | Fixed 50% per round | Affected by spot, choice, and fight flow |
| Failure | Execute vanilla scavenging | Handled per current voyage action result |
| Fish pool | Differs by odd/even day; some fish gated by intel | Varies by planet, location, and unlock conditions |

Linguan and Caststar fish still require the corresponding intel; king fish still require story and advanced gear. Choosing the trait does not cancel voyages, nor does it mean scavenging can unconditionally obtain all fish.

## Farming & Storage

### Prepare the Environment First

Before farming, check the terminal fish file to confirm diet, growth stage, water need, and nocturnal waste. The system reuses the vanilla growth, feeding, water quality, and breeding base flow; each species' specific parameters differ.

| Factor | Actual meaning | Advice |
|---|---|---|
| Space | Whether fish and offspring physically fit | Leave room for growth changes and spawning |
| Water | Satisfy the fish's current-stage need | Fill the tank with water first, then place fish in slots |
| Filtration | Pollution-handling capacity | A bigger tank cannot replace stronger filtration |
| Food | Sustain growth and breeding | Provide edible feed by diet |
| Power | Equipment functioning | Check power and battery regularly |

The large ecological farming device's display spec is 24×4 tiles with 4 filter slots, supporting expansion and power-saving upgrades; the water tank is purchased separately. The 60 L tank is created empty and must be filled. Old tanks only update capacity on load, keeping existing water, not auto-filling. Actual device state follows the current panel.

Do not calculate capacity only by juvenile size. A fish's weight, water use, and waste may change after entering a new stage, and adult age does not equal maximum weight. For example, black fish's current adult node is 13 development days; small yellow croaker and sardine are 4 development days; actual nights elapsed also depend on feeding and growth speed.

### Source, Grade & Breeding

Fish grown from eggs with a preserved source can show as farmed; fish obtained at sea are wild; old-save fish with unknown source are not forcibly recognized. Source and species participate in processing, research, and pricing, so do not treat same-named old fish meat as known-source samples.

Whole-fish grade is fixed at first catch; fillet main meat inherits grade; bred fish default to grade 4. Sea fish spawn at the species' adult stage; traits are applied only at first catch. Each collected fish grants 10 fishing XP; max level requires 9500 XP.

Breeding normally requires an adult male and female in the same tank, with food and environmental needs met; overnight there is a chance of spawning. Two adult fish do not guarantee daily spawn, and high grade cannot substitute for water and food conditions.

Markup accumulates step by step from new weight actually fed in the farming device — it is not granted instantly upon placing the fish in the tank. Weight recovered after prior loss does not earn again; live fish and the meat filleted from it share one farming-progress ledger to avoid double-markup. After reaching the main-growth cap, new weight in the adult-to-elder stage can still earn tail markup at a lower rate, up to half the main-stage extra markup.

For example, black fish's main-growth markup goes from 1× stepwise up to 4×; with the tail stage fully earned, the theoretical cap is 5.5×. Swordfish's configured cap is 1×, positioned toward large meat and feed supply rather than chasing high-profit fish via farming premium. These multipliers describe only the farming-markup layer; absolute profit still depends on weight, grade, base price, per-piece rounding, feed and equipment cost, and does not equal daily net profit.

"Farmed / wild" is a source label; the label from egg growth is separate from actual feeding-weight markup. Do not infer from the label alone that a fish has earned full markup.

### Freezer & Worms

The freezer stores food and live fish. A live fish's physiological state pauses when placed inside and resumes when taken out, so it is suitable for temporarily holding breeders or samples; while paused it cannot be treated as an active farm tank.

The worm breeding box accepts worms, fish meat, and fish offal. Fish meat and offal convert to box stored-reserve overnight by real calories, consumed only when live worms are present. Materials still lying in slots are not yet spendable built-in reserve.

Juvenile worms need sufficiently-fed nights to grow; adult worms can be cut by existing operations or paired to breed. Check age, satiety, and space prompts; dead worms cannot be used as live breeders.

Two feed buttons produce industrial feed and industrial meat feed respectively. Each craft consumes 1 surviving adult worm and 5000 kcal stored reserve, producing one 5000 kcal feed, delivered to warehouse or main inventory with suitable space. When output cannot fit or crafting fails, preserve and restore inputs. Industrial meat feed is an independent meat food item, used per vanilla feed mechanics, and cannot be split.

## Filleting & By-products

Filleting splits meat by the species' calorie tiers — not all fish cut into the same size. Configuration is based on meat weight per 100 kcal, with actual meat tiers defined per species; for example, some fish use 400/300/200/100 kcal four tiers, others may have larger tiers. Do not read 100 kcal as meaning all cuts are fixed at 100 kcal.

The system allocates calories from large to small tiers; remaining calories reaching 25 kcal are kept as fish offal; a remainder below 25 kcal produces no offal. Fillet main meat preserves source and grade. Each produced cut has its own calories and grid footprint; this version no longer provides the old meat-combining flow.

By-products are counted independently by whole-fish weight: quantity = floor(fish weight ÷ grams per unit), unrelated to how many cuts were produced. Currently 23 ordinary biological fish have per-species fillet tiers; 12 of them have extra by-product rules. Great white shark and Emperor red-armor crab go through their own decomposition flows.

## Drying

Place a recipe-supported fish into the drying rack and wait the days shown on the rack. Different fish have independent recipes; unsupported fish will not start just by being placed. After a day advance, completed fish may show as pending conversion; open the rack to complete conversion.

The new flow preserves the fish's body weight and energy; "sale-yield base" is used only for pricing and does not mean actual body and calories shrink proportionally. Finished products carry a dried marker and cannot be dried again or filleted again. Decide before drying if you need meat or by-products.

Base price is computed from sale-yield weight and the recipe's per-kg price, then crystallize and grade modifiers are applied. Crystallization corresponds to 1.5×; grades 4, 3, 2, 1, special correspond to 1, 1.1, 1.25, 1.5, 2× respectively. These are price modifiers, not extra meat or by-products.

## Canned Fish

### Canning & Methods

Double-click a small or large empty can to load whole fish or fish meat. The small can interior is 4×3, the large 6×4; how much fits is limited first by shape and grid.

You can add no seasoning, or choose one of salt, spicy, or oil routes. Up to 2 portions of the same route per can; salt, chili, and fish oil cannot be mixed into multiple methods. Salt and super salt belong to the salt route; chili and blazing chili to the spicy route; fish oil to the oil route.

### Quality & Price

| Scoring item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Base score | Highest base among all fish ingredients: whole fish 1, fish meat 2; counted once |
| Suitability | Fish ingredients matching the method add by count, not total weight |
| Seasoning | Adds by portion count, capped by the two-portion seasoning limit |
| Mixed fish | Mixing different species −1 |
| Final quality | Clamped to 1–5 |

The salt route suits water-purifier fish, glass fish, rainbow fish, blue crystal fish, Luo Fu Ling, Neolon, ferrite jellyfish, etc.; the spicy route includes black fish, mouse fish, great white shark, swordfish, Emperor red-armor crab, volcanic fish, Muspel's Son, red-tail fish, Wu Zhi Qi, Rahn's Nightmare, etc.; remaining species are mostly the oil route, and vanilla fish also have their own mapping. Unknown-source old fish meat does not guess species suitability.

Finished value uses the base value of the empty can and all input materials, multiplied by the quality coefficient: grades 1–5 are 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, 1.4, 1.5×, then rounded. Small empty can base value 5, large 10. Whether adding expensive material to gain a grade is worth it requires comparing actual input, not just the quality number.

### Processing & Can Rack

Once curing starts, the material window locks, with a base processing time of 2 days; the can rack accelerates, check completion via the interface progress. Prepare output space that fits the product; when it cannot fit, the source can is kept and can be retried per the flow.

Cans are meat food and can be placed into existing can racks. Storage no longer ages them for value, so indefinite placement does not indefinitely appreciate. Old sealed cans are only kept as placeholders for old saves, no longer used under the old merchandise/processing play.

### Canned Calculation Example

Two pieces of ordinary fish meat suitable for the current route, plus one matching seasoning: fish meat base 2 + two-piece suitability 2 + seasoning 1 = 5 points. Using meat unsuitable for the route loses those two suitability points; mixing another species deducts 1 more. If the empty can and all materials total 100 in value, a grade-5 can's processed value is 150, and storage time does not increase this number.

## Container Printing

Pay 300 credits at Willie's processing service to unlock container printing, and receive 1 container printer. After unlocking, the corresponding equipment becomes purchasable; once you have the printer you can use it with no further gate.

Drag a battery into the designated battery slot; each craft consumes 4 power and produces 1 item. You can print small empty cans, large empty cans, worm breeding boxes, and empty syringes. Check remaining power and output space before printing; on low power or no fit, no resources should be consumed. If the first free gift fails to deliver, handle pending or recovery prompts first.

## Processing Station

Double-click the processing station to open its panel, place the recipe's materials into the input slots, and craft. The full 58-recipe table is in chapter 9 of the [Detailed Stats Guide](<Go Fishing 2.5.1 Detailed Stats Guide.md>).

Matching rule: the material types must exactly match a recipe (extra, missing, or unrelated materials will not match), and each material only needs to reach the recipe's minimum count — it does not have to equal it exactly. With surplus materials you can craft multiple outputs at once: the plus/minus buttons around the count box switch freely between 1 and 10 portions regardless of current stock; the check against materials happens when you click start, and a mismatched recipe or insufficient materials shows a notice instead of failing silently. The station only accepts the material forms a recipe names — for example, the fish meat mash recipes take whole small yellow croakers or sardines, while most advanced recipes take fish organs and processed materials such as conductive tendons, heat glands, and purification sacs; a whole fish of another kind will not match.

Above the count selector there is a note-style manual slot where the processing manual can be placed; clicking the manual in the slot opens the illustrated recipe book (16 pages, 58 recipes, browsed by category). The manual also works without being slotted — just double-click the book itself.

## Fishmeal & Reagents

### Making a base solution with the Blender

Attach a liquid container to the Blender and blend fish scraps with an eligible species origin. The base solution goes straight into the container and follows the game's capacity and overflow rules. Higher module tiers reduce extraction loss. Scraps from Purifier Fish make a good example for a single-effect detox reagent. The first equipment grant includes a Blender. Legacy grinders retain their inventories and previously queued output.

The five legacy dry powders can still be used. Loading an old save converts their weight and grade into native chemical components. Dissolving consumes the whole portion under the game's overflow rules. Base solutions can also be poured between containers.

### Mixing, balancing and filling

1. Put the container in the reagent station's base slot, select an amount and review the live preview of effect values, stability and side-effect tiers, then confirm to add it to the batch.
2. Add a more concentrated base when an effect is too weak. Add water to dilute an overly strong batch and check whether the effect drops a tier. For a dual-effect batch, establish the main effect first and add the second base in small amounts.
3. Choose a balancing target, review additive doses and the projected profile, then confirm. Measure additives per 10 mL of base before additives. Each complete A+B pair lowers its target adverse effect by one tier. Stabilizer uses a single treatment. See the in-game manual or the Detailed Stats Guide for pairs.
4. Supply empty syringes and power. Each syringe takes exactly 10 mL and uses one empty syringe and one charge. Any leftover solution stays in the station.
5. The filling panel shows the dose count, remainder and base price per syringe. Save successful formulas to history and supply the actual ingredients when repeating them.

The seven-page Reagent Manual includes a fish-scrap source page before the additive reference and walks through a Purifier Fish detox recipe and includes concentration and pricing references.

## Crystal Nautilites

### Trader and purchasing

Visit intervals use the game day when the next visit is scheduled: 3–4 days on days 1–14, 2–3 days on days 15–29, and 1–2 days from day 30 onward. Existing scheduled visits keep their dates.

The nautilite trader brings 4–6 stones from Aurora or Linguan, subject to available desk space. Each stone takes up a 2×2 area. Raw stones have a base asking price of 300 credits and reveal their type and grade when opened. Opened stones are quoted by type and grade.

Each opened stone has a 30% chance of arriving with one cut. You can see its actual sprite before buying, but the cut is shown as “?” in the details and the trader quotes it as uncut. A bare-stone cut has no colored overlay. The result is revealed upon purchase and counts toward the three-cut limit. Willy visits the day after your first purchase to give you the illustrated four-page guide A Veteran’s Experience. Its fourth page compares Chlorite and Green Fluorite cores over a Common stone at three opacity levels.

### Opening, cutting and polishing

1. Inspect a stone you own with the magnifying glass. Open a raw stone to reveal its type (Salt, Toxic or Iridescent) and grade (Low Grade, Common, Fine or Collector Grade).
2. Drag the built-in grinder and keep its white tip in contact with a cut spot for a total of two seconds. Cutting is free, with one cut per spot and up to three per stone. Color and quality clues appear during grinding. The result is saved when the cut completes. Bare stone reduces value, low-grade minerals vary by type, and gems increase value.
3. Select **Polish** to finish the stone. The marker travels out and back in four seconds, two seconds each way. The three green zones vary in width and are randomly placed. Confirm once per zone on either pass. A mistimed click leaves a crack immediately, and each zone still missed at the end leaves one crack.
4. Once polishing starts, cutting is locked and you must finish before selling or bartering. Reopening restores the layout and scored zones. Zero, one, two or three cracks yield 120%, 95%, 75% or 60% of the current appraisal, respectively.

You can sell a stone before starting polish. All nautilites are materials. A revealed gem core adds the gemstone tag, and an opened stone with an appraisal above 1,000 credits qualifies as a luxury item. For quotes, odds and valuation, see chapter 11 of the [Detailed Stats Guide](<Go Fishing 2.5.1 Detailed Stats Guide.md>).

## Research Codex & Integrations

With the fishing terminal and qualified samples, you can research per planet conditions — no need to unlock the farming system first. Submitting research consumes 1 owned fish; dried fish is not accepted. Decide the purpose of rare species, breeders, and unique quest samples before clicking submit.

The codex shows catch counts, max weight, traits, first-acquisition time, and filleting records; the farming file shows stage, weight, water need, and waste, suitable for review before expanding farming. Actual files are more reliable than applying one fish's experience to all.

Fishing Cuisine Expansion provides the cooking gameplay. Its recipes define which processed fish, dried fish, cans and seasonings each dish accepts.
