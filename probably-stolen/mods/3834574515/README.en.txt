FORBIDDEN REGALIA — THIS MIGHT KILL YOU
Version 1.5.10 · Probably Stolen 0.46D

77 rebuildable watches from 13 fictional makers, sealed collector’s pieces, 30 modular guns, six high-tech melee weapons, workshop commissions, night shops and specialist visitors.

INSTALLATION
Use the game’s native mod loader. Chinese, English and Russian are included; restart and reload after changing the game language. Continue 1.x saves; old 0.x saves are incompatible. Enable only one copy, local or Workshop. The release has no Ctrl+F7 developer panel.

GETTING STARTED
The Regalia Ledger under the counter contains visitor profiles, recipes, supply routes, appraisal references, prices and station-politics guidance. Existing item identity, condition, provenance and orders are preserved. Prepaid orders, held bids and accepted contracts retain their agreed terms; inventory reference values follow the updated catalogue.
Start a watch business with the workbench, loupe and analyzer. The Watchmakers’ Guild opens on day 3, the Unmarked Workshop on day 5. Check other pages on the night map. Move goods from the shelves to the trade tray, pay through the native checkout and use Return to leave.
The Guild stocks one precision workbench per day (1,950 credits); the Unmarked Workshop stocks one basic bench (195 credits). On her first visit, Li Wu gives you a precision bench if you own a basic one, or a basic bench if you own neither. Owning a precision bench prevents a repeat gift. Workbenches handle assembly, adaptation and customization; stamping now uses the dedicated labeller.

WATCH TRADING
Regular watches have four components: movement, dial, case and strap. Compare parts against factory references with the loupe, or spend electricity to measure authenticity and condition directly. The analyzer accepts individual parts and whole watches.
The workbench handles assembly and compatibility. Six signature combinations have recipes in the Ledger. Sealed collector’s pieces are inspected and traded as whole watches.
Restore worn parts or fabricate replacements using materials, power and a processing fee. Some gold parts require ingots; replicas made with precious metals remain replicas. Stamp individual parts or an entire watch to change its declared identity. Deceiving buyers can result in repayment demands, lost reputation and criminal charges.
Display the Trade, Appraisal, Servicing or Repair sign to attract the corresponding business. Commission payments depend on the work completed.

SOURCING AND AUCTIONS
Use the Watchmakers’ Contact Card to register all 13 factory contacts. Orders are prepaid in full and cannot be cancelled after confirmation. Each brand ships one batch per week; T1 requires five units, T2 two, T3 one. T4/T5 require VIP history, allocation credit and weekly stock. Secondhand sources have no such restrictions. Calling again does not reroll stock or lead times.
Unlisted Auctions offer a new lot each week, including sealed watches, counterfeit Prismatic Regalia and rare materials. Remote inspection uses zoomable photos only. Each lot accepts one binding bid, held in full. Results arrive the next day: unsuccessful bids are refunded; winners pay their bid. Delivery remains pending if storage is full. Check the newspaper supplement for the lot and market news.

STARTING AN ARMS BUSINESS
Melee upgrades proceed through inlay, amplifier and core, with four separate appearances per weapon and larger footprints at higher stages. Remove upgrades in reverse order. Ion cores fit T2+, Ember T4+, Yuan T5. Existing components are retained; missing earlier stages must be installed before later bonuses activate. Move tightly packed old weapons to an open area and reopen their upgrade window to apply the new footprint.
Visit Broken Rail Armory from day 5. Buy the native 3D printer, Metal Printing and CNC modules, a battery, recharger, production chips and materials. Existing equipment works too.
Native printers and all three fabricators accept chips of any tier, provided the materials and finished item fit. Broken Rail Armory sells the fabricators. T3/T4/T5 offer 1.5/2/3 times native base speed, +10%/+20%/+30% manufactured stats for this mod’s complete guns and melee weapons, base power draws of 16/24/40 kJ per cycle, and 48/63/80 module cells. Modules and starting traits still apply. The native input tray stays 9×6; advanced fabricators offer larger input, output and module areas. Install the modules and power supply, place a reusable chip in its slot, add the recipe’s metals and feedstock, and leave space for output. Each cycle consumes materials and electricity. Remove the chip or materials when finished. Five feedstock grades have distinct artwork and use 2×2 inventory space. Metals and exotic elements are specified by each recipe.
Use the native modification window to fit matching optics, feed systems, stocks and front-end attachments. Appearance, footprint and stats change together. Move the gun to an open area if there is insufficient space. Weapon stats support trade, orders and related systems. High-tech melee weapons use their own attributes and upgrades.
Display Arms Trade, Custom Orders, Heavy Weapons or Parts signs to attract relevant business. Yan Ji, Liao Yin, Shen Li, Qi Ya and Zero Chord bring stock and crafting materials according to progress and relationships. Higher-tier goods and chips have separate access requirements; consult the Ledger.

CONNECTIONS, LOOT AND REGALIA
Gold Viper, Crimson Falcon and Bai E buy complete high-end weapons carrying both Luxury and Contraband eligibility. Mod guns start at T3; real-condition requirements are 85, 75 and 80 respectively. Item tooltips show eligibility. Good weapons improve their next haul. Stolen stock retains its provenance and legal risk; legitimate supply offers lower-risk turnover.
Support Bai E with capsule gifts or amber coffin treatment. She does not buy capsules. Gifting transfers the whole stack: +1 growth per gift and 3 stable days per capsule, subject to the daily growth cap. Recovery and relationships unlock better stock, concealed storage and nighttime protection. Stable health, favor 4 and growth 3 unlock auction raids. The confirmation shows weapon thresholds, odds, medicine and fees. Committed weapons, fitted attachments and expenses are not refunded. A successful raid delivers the actual lot with its identity and condition intact and 100% stolen-goods exposure.
Relationships unlock the assassins’ evidence services; dialogue shows costs and cooldowns. Crime values, investigations and police disruption follow their respective rules. Consult the active service and status panels.
Forbidden Court Essence invites Ying Xiyuan. Qualifying sought-after watches or counterfeit Regalia may also attract her. Relationships and royal builds open access to her collection. Her coffin, mirror, throne and sword have practical uses described in the Ledger. Unregistered Yuan Matter can trigger a claim: returning it earns favor, trusted clients can pay to register it, and confirming refusal ends the run.

WALL DISPLAYS AND NIGHTTIME POWER
Earn one gun rack and one sword rack through story progress. Installation fixes them to the wall. Click a mount to manage weapons; display enables deterrence, while concealment protects rack contents and pauses display effects. A displayed divine sword turns inspectors and sentencing officers away; existing cases remain.
Yuan Matter, Ember cores and Regalia recharge eligible batteries overnight, subject to placement, upkeep and daily limits. Take batteries out of bags, hidden compartments and ordinary containers, and place them in suitable shop positions. Yuan Matter can also act as an initially empty 200kJ slow-charging power source. Registration and claims still apply. See the Ledger power pages for exact rules.

OPTIONAL STATION POLITICS
Join explicitly to activate the system. Completed equipment sales to named factions affect strength, tensions, conflict, disorder, markets and procurement. Regular-military supply helps de-escalation; postwar restocking creates further demand. Official requisition orders state their conditions and current exemption. Private sales follow normal rules. Players who do not join can continue watch trading, repairs and ordinary shopkeeping.

PRICES AND SAVES
The Ledger separates base values, sourcing costs and special premiums. Base reference values: divine sword 500,000; unique original Prismatic Regalia 300,000; coffin 12,000; mirror 18,000; throne 30,000. Actual offers also depend on condition, authenticity, channel and market. Calculate factory-allocation profit across the entire buying cycle.
The mod ID remains local.forbidden_guests. Continue with the newer version after upgrading; use a pre-upgrade save backup when rolling back. Dispose of mod items before uninstalling. AI-assisted art; fictional brands and models.


Arms progression (1.5.7): T3/T4/T5 fabricator base prices are 2,000/7,000/18,000. T1/T2 firearm work is 30/60; attachments 12/24. 1.5.8 — 典藏流通与军械库修复 / Collectibles and Armory Fixes

• 全部封存典藏原装在玩家成功入手后停止生成第二件，商人、暗拍与抢拍共用记录。私改典藏入手后冷却28天，仿品照常供应。

• 军械库材料按厂商分架，通用金属单独分类，材料说明补充厂商和等级。
• T3/T4/T5武器制作箱占格调整为2×2、3×3、4×4；内部容量、插槽和制作性能不变。旧箱子读档后同步缩小。
• 扩大军械库结账区，修复大型设备无法结账的问题；离店清空后恢复。
• 更新中英文说明及御藏名录。旧档会补登记现有典藏及可追溯的交易、拍卖与抢拍记录；已流失且没有记录的旧典藏无法追溯，现有物品不会被删除。

• Each genuine sealed collectible is unique per save once acquired. Dealers, auctions and raids share the same ownership history. Customized variants have a 28-day restock cooldown; replicas remain available.
• Ying Xiyuan accepts stolen genuine watches that meet her buying requirements, paying 75% of the normal negotiated price. Goods she actually buys add no new transaction-related crime. Existing cases and stolen-item markers remain.
• Armory materials now have separate manufacturer shelves, with shared metals in their own category. Descriptions identify the manufacturer and grade.
• T3/T4/T5 fabricators now occupy 2×2, 3×3 and 4×4 cells. Internal storage, module space and performance are unchanged. Existing fabricators shrink when the save loads.
• Expanded the armory checkout tray so large equipment can be purchased. The original tray size is restored after leaving with an empty tray.
• Updated Chinese and English text and the Regalia Ledger. Existing saves register current collectibles and recoverable trade, auction and raid records. Previously lost items without surviving records cannot be reconstructed; existing items are preserved.
