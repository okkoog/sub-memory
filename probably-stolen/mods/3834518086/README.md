# Brewing & Winemaking

Brew beer and make new wines from new crops in this expansion mod. Automate your business with new machines: the Brewery, Winery, Glass Printer, and Bottling Line.

### Brew beer:
- Brew 9 beers, graded by their ingredients
- Brew Barley Ale, Rust Cider, Nectar Ale and Hydrohop IPA, the best ordinary beer
- Dream Cap Beer is contraband and sells high

### Make wine:
- Make 9 new wines: Sourcider, Pearry, Bloompop, Duskvine Red, Dusk Rosé, and more!
- Nutricooler from Nutrifruit, by hand or Winery, graded by fruit size
- Grandmaster Vintage is a wine above Master Vintage, for those who find the recipe
- Some say a Grandmaster bottle never stops getting better
- Quality Yeast is a yeast bred for better wine
- Reusable Super Yeast and Reusable Quality Yeast are never used up, and rare

### Age your wine:
- Age Well Expansion Kits add two rows to an Age Well
- The Age Well Hopper Port is a slot for an outlet hopper that feeds the wine grid
- Sold at Dr. Jackson's once you own the Winemaker upgrade

### Grow crops:
- 6 new hydroponic crops: Duskgrape, Rustapple, Smogpear, Nectarbloom, Hydrohop and Lumen Cap
- Duskgrape, Rustapple and Smogpear fruit again and again
- Seeds sold in [Better Merchants](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3834518042)' shops

### Automate:
- The Bottling Line hosts a Brewery or Winery and fills bottles every night
- The Glass Printer prints the bottles it needs, inside the line or on its own
- Hoppers load and unload the Bottling Line, and it bottles all it can in one night
- The Bottling Line's input and output grids are 12 wide
- The Winery and Brewery take reusable yeasts in slots
- Sold at Dr. Jackson's once you own the Winemaker upgrade

All settings are under the gear button in the Mods menu.

### What's new in 1.2.0:
- Wider Bottling Line, bottling all in one night
- Reusable yeast slots, and one yeast is enough at the Winery
- Age Well Expansion Kit and Hopper Port
- One Quality Yeast of either kind is enough
- 3 beers and 9 wines from 6 crops, Lumen Cap now 2x2

## Mod compatibility
Should work with these mods:
- [Brewing Expansion](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3829730904): our wines come only from our crops, its fruits make its wines
- [More Device Upgrades](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3831439549): it sets the Age Well's size, so our Expansion Kit is refused and not sold while it runs

Not compatible with these mods:
- Mods that replace the fermentation start or finish step: Quality Yeast bonus, wine styles and Nutricooler results may be off

---

Safe to add to an existing save. Empty every Bottling Line (hoppers and slotted yeast too) and Beer Case before removing this mod, and take the outlet hopper out of each Age Well's port and empty each upgraded Age Well. Needs no permissions. English, Chinese, French (French translation by Dreams) and Russian.
