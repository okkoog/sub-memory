Neon Undertow | Chemistry at the counter
Workshop release 0.5.13

GETTING STARTED

At the Glassmarket Counter & Fixture Stall, look under Shop goods. It carries
three of the twelve Undertow stock compounds each day. All twelve rotate
through in four days. An individual stock cylinder contains 6 mL. You can
still finish the original stocks on their own, or save them to make a mix.

For a first sale, buy Dock Chalk and Emberwake on the first day of a rotation.
Pour both stocks into the same cylinder and add a little water. On the native
heating plate, heat the mixture to at least 65 °C and give it time to react.
The composition spectrometer can identify Yardglass in the mixture. Use a
charged evaporator to remove the water. Move the cylinder to the Chem
Finisher, select Yardglass, and finish a package. A narcotics buyer with
sufficient interest and budget can buy it at the counter.

customer's budget can matter more than their interest in a higher-priced item.

SPLITTING A BATCH

Keep a cylinder of the reacted Seam, Bridge or Daybreak base. Split it
before packaging. Each half can take a different additive. For a first try,
combine both 6 mL stock cylinders from a first-stage card and 4 mL of water.
The spectrometer should show 12 mL of active and 4 mL of water after heating.
Move 8 mL to a clean cylinder using the vanilla 5 mL and 1 mL droppers. Each
half now has 6 mL active and 2 mL water. Put 6 mL of carrot juice in one
Seam cylinder and 6 mL of Glass
Choir stock in the other. Heat each to at least 55 °C until the new active
appears. Finish the first wet for Stitchfilm. Evaporate the second and
finish it for Afterimage.

Use one listed additive per base. Two competing additives in the same
cylinder prevent that base from converting. A minimum 0.5 mL of process water
is required in addition to any water in the juice or milk. Milk recipes
consume milk fat, protein, sugar and the milk marker. Milk containing microbes
cannot drive the reaction while those microbes are present. The Clinic
Dipstrip tests only for microbes.

Stitchfilm reduces the severity of a stabilized wound. The lozenge and
taper temporarily suppress active nicotine and alcohol withdrawal,
respectively. The Sentinel Buffer uses the regular Immunivax response and
its genuine-injector base value of 160 credits. Check the pharmacist's budget.
Drag the serum onto a sick rat or the salve onto an injured living fish.
The swab distinguishes ordinary rat disease from prion carriage. The serum
does not treat prions. Burrow Balm restores up to 20 health to an injured
rat. Use the Trauma Pack on a serious unstable wound to stabilize it and
reduce its severity by two points. Dip the clinic strip into a vessel for a microbial
reading. The Brood Wafer is a small 10-calorie feed portion. Afterimage
goes to narcotics buyers.

BENCH SUPPLIES AND RESTRICTED GOODS

For Orchard Seal blend apple and lemon separately. For Reedbed Sachet use
pear and celery. Heat 20 mL of each matching juice in a 50 mL lab cylinder
at 45 °C. Only the blender's juice solids react. The 0.4 mL of active left
after evaporation covers a 0.3 mL pack. The 25, 50 and 100 mL graduated
cylinders all support the reaction when the chosen quantities fit.

Use Orchard Seal to restore up to 15 durability on a worn, empty Undertow
soda container. The maximum durability, rust and dents are preserved.
Use Reedbed Sachet on a watery sample to remove up to 1 mL of plant matter.
It leaves microbes and dissolved chemicals in the vessel.

For Ghost Ink, combine Undertint active with Mirror Salt stock. Finish 2 mL
wet at the usual branch temperature. This restricted assay tool reveals the
condition of an owned, unexamined injector. It is sold as a tool and carries
a contraband flag separately from narcotics.

YARDGLASS LAB ACCIDENT

A dry Yardglass cook in an owned graduated cylinder can kill the player.
On HIGH, a rattling warning starts when both fictional stock ingredients
are present in quantity and the sample overheats. Switch off, switch to
LOW or remove the cylinder before four more hazardous machine ticks pass.
Once the native countdown starts, the blast ends the run. Properly hydrated
batches, other mixtures and simply selecting a recipe cannot start it.

RESEARCH BEHIND THE DESIGN

FAO identifies apple pomace and citrus peel as rich sources of pectin.
That inspired the fictional Orchard Seal binder and using only fruit solids
for its in-game yield. https://www.fao.org/4/W6864E/w6864e08.htm

EPA describes suspended-solids removal and disinfection as separate steps.
Reedbed implements solids removal alone.
https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPURL.cgi?Dockey=P100NGKH.TXT

A device submission hosted by FDA describes absorbent calcium alginate
dressings supplied as pads or ribbons. That distinction informed the
packing roll's role in the game. Crop recipes, health thresholds, numerical
effects, narcotics and the Yardglass accident are fictional game design.
https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf23/K233111.pdf

WHERE TO EXTEND THE SYSTEM

QuayChemistry.cs holds the first-stage mix pairs. QuayBranches.cs lists
the base, additive, format and purpose for downstream recipes. CounterStock.cs
registers both stages with the existing liquid and Chem Finisher directories.
The Workshop Fieldbook covers both stages in all eight localization files.

For another formula, pick an obtainable pair, a distinct use or buyer, and
an existing output sprite. Check the shelf rotation and the cost per batch.
Give it a stable item and liquid ID. A new formula must fit in the native
selector, retain its composition through a save, and earn an actual buyer
payment. If a pair shares a reagent with another reaction, specify which
products a vessel containing all three stocks should make before enabling it.

A possible later addition is a hospital order for a specific purity grade.
