NEON UNDERTOW 0.14.1 - BOUNTY FILES AND SODA ART

Six wanted sheets, nine people per sheet: Butcher Warrants, Military Fugitives,
Smugglers, Saboteurs, Fraudsters and Unknown Bounties. All 54 people have their
own arrival, questioning, trading, arrest and return dialogue.

Officer Lun visits with the first sheet in a small book-sized Bounty Folder.
He brings later sheets on follow-up visits, at least three days apart. Each
new sheet unlocks its nine visitors after the normal wanted-introduction gate.
Double-click the folder, then activate a sheet. Keep the folder beside your
books, inside the book cubby or inside the vanilla Dossier. The wanted papers
use the game's usual screen, reward fields and red capture crosses.

Rewards range from 150 to 2,500 credits, before native trait modifiers. Security
pays them through the normal arrest sequence. Captured people cannot return or
pay a second reward. People you release can return; seven can give you a card
and a native phone-book contact. Call to arrange another visit when available.
Dahlia Vey offers estate weapons at a 200% markup (three times ordinary price).

Twenty soda flavours now have curved labels on small cans, large cans and glass
bottles. Worn and damaged cans keep the corrected designs. Empty cans use clean
neutral metal. Bottles hold 750 mL and use normal liquid controls: pour, drink,
empty or refill them. Their labels stay attached while fill and colour follow
the actual contents. Bottled sodas are stocked at the Co-op and food suppliers.

Everything is included in the main Neon Undertow mod. No bounty addon or second
Workshop subscription is required.
