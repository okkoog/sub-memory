Neon Undertow 0.13.0

KITCHEN: open the back-room KITCHEN worktop. Choose an instrument; put only one ingredient combination, water and a charged cell in INPUT. LOAD consumes ingredients/resources. Every action advances five seconds. Set heat, work/turn/stir and finish at the doneness shown. Books are technique references. Closing pauses cooking; unfinished batches save with the worktop. Food cannot be instantly crafted or automatically cooked.

CELLAR: buy a Cellar Bench, Brewing Notes, clean bottles and culture from the Canning Co-op CELLAR shelf. Fermentation takes three in-game nights, distillation one, infusions two. Full outputs retain finished batches. Use the CELLAR tab for up to fourteen nights of maturation. All twenty fruits, twenty vegetables and eight grains are used. Bottle art and alcohol consumption use the base game.

COMPATIBILITY: originals are optional. Keep Brewing/Tobacco enabled when loading a save containing their items. No automatic conversion/removal of third-party items. Existing food IDs, soda art and compact table are preserved.
