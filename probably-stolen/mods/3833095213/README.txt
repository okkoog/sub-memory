Neon Undertow 0.15.11

The manual bottler takes up 3 x 3 inventory cells. Bring the Bottling Handbook, high-quality water (99% or higher), one matching concentrate and an empty glass bottle (750 mL) or can (350 or 500 mL). The bottle you put in is the bottle you get back. The Quayside Canning Co-op sells all three concentrates and stocked supply cases.

Food reserves the grid cells under its artwork. Supply cases open as fixed grids, and their purchased contents can be moved out and back in. Rat coats, fish patterns and nursing milk are tied to the individual animal. The rat start now includes standard coat varieties. Fish sold as fertilized eggs carry a clutch with its own colours. At the Co-op, select FISH + CARE and then NEXT SPECIES for eels, sturgeon and the other lines.

STARTUP HOTFIX: handles game-language changes during startup. See CHANGELOG-0.15.2.txt and TROUBLESHOOTING.txt. The main release includes 54 bounty visitors on six sheets, updated sodas, inherited rat traits and fish breeding across six species. Unfinished tool/workshop expansion remains excluded.

HANDBOOKS: the corner guide is off by default. Mods > Neon Undertow > Settings > Show corner guide restores a smaller optional shortcut immediately. Four physical Undertow handbooks use the vanilla book reader, index and page arrows; their last page is saved. A small movable book cubby arrives once on the MAIN counter when there is space, for both new and existing saves. It occupies 3 x 2 cells and stores books/documents in an 8 x 6 grid. Keep it beside your other books or move it, still full, into the vanilla Dossier to organize your documents. Its picture shows whether it contains books. Replacement guides and empty cubbies are on the Co-op KITCHEN + BOOKS shelf. Recipe books still have to be bought/earned and moved into the relevant machine INPUT.

Opened drinks can be tested with the usual liquid-testing tools.

OLDER DRINKS: sealed 750 mL Quayside Reserve, Dockside Cordial and Nightshift Tonic can be opened into a native liquid container, returning a reusable bottle. New crafted batches retain source components. Previous sealed batches lack composition records: their declared 99.5% source minimum is retained and the remainder is marked unrecorded.

WATER GENERATOR: end-tier four-bay machine, sold at Stormglass Depot Outlet from day 28 with a Grand Waterworks installed. List price 12,500 credits before reputation discounts. Generates four new native 2 L bottles of native high-quality water per day, using 6 power each (24 for all four). No water, empty-bottle or concentrate input. Insufficient power/space reduces production; missed output is not banked. Starting traits cannot grant it. Existing machines retain obsolete input/attachment contents in withdraw-only recovery panels that disappear when empty.

KITCHEN: buy reusable boards, bowls, pans and powered instruments from Quayside. Place the actual instrument on the back-room worktop with one ingredient combination and listed water/power. Select it on COOK. Pans and saucepans need an owned galley or native heating plate; chilling needs an owned native fridge. Other recipes no longer depend on unrelated crate racks or Waterworks tiers. LOAD consumes ingredients/resources, and each action advances five seconds. Manage heat, tending and finishing time. Closing pauses the saved batch. Food cannot be instantly crafted or automated.

BOOKS & PRODUCTION: activate recipe books to read them in the native paper notebook. Their blueprints still work in native crafting tables; the kitchen also recognizes native books. Keep a Fizzworks Recipe Book in canner INPUT, or Workshop Recipes / Production Handbook in galley INPUT. Choose recipes through the native item chooser. Book removal stops production. Metal cans/tins use three new chips in the ordinary printer, with one metal ingot, Metal Printing and CNC modules and charged power. Native printer progress applies. Buy the chips at Undertow Engineering; buy empty packaging at Quayside when starting out.

CELLAR: buy a Cellar Bench, Brewing Notes, clean bottles and culture from the Canning Co-op CELLAR shelf. The 76 recipes use suitable fruit, grain, starch crops and infusion ingredients; starch recipes require their listed conversion ingredient. Full outputs retain finished batches. CELLAR is bottle storage. Use the native powered Age-Well rack to mature wines, cider and perry, capped at fourteen aging steps; native performance modules affect the rack. Bottled spirits do not gain age value. Cask rye includes oak maturation in its recipe. Previous drink IDs remain supported.

BAR: Glassmarket stocks a compact tool rail, native measures and blender, mixing glass and spoon, capped shaker, reusable bottles and a readable bartender's notebook. Use ordinary inventory targeting to pour through a measure into a mixing tool. Activate that tool to stir or shake, then pour into a serving vessel. Tools work in the rail's slots or your main inventory. The rail has a fixed stocked illustration; open its slots to see the actual contents. Tools are sold separately.

LIQUIDS: all 76 drinks can be combined. Native bottles, jugs and tanks preserve the mixture when transferring it. Clear, amber and green bottles are reusable; their liquid level and colour change, while their printed labels stay attached. The ordinary blender extracts juices from all fruits and six suitable vegetables. Open sodas into containers using the can, keeping the empty can and its wear. Updated soda labels and bare cans are included.

STANDALONE: Neon Undertow includes its own Cellar Bench, 76 brewing recipes and bar systems. No other mods are required. Existing food and drink IDs, artwork and saved items are preserved.
