NEON UNDERTOW: NPC TOTAL OVERHAUL + MORE — 0.12.0

Enable Neon Undertow and the original Brewing Expansion (3829730904) and
Tobacco Expansion (3832840208), then restart Probably Stolen. No activation
key is needed. The NEON UNDERTOW · ACTIVE button opens the content guide.

MEGABLOCK KITCHEN
Quayside Canning Co-op sells eight grain portions, four raw meat portions,
the five kitchen books, fruit, vegetables, fizz charges and soda botanicals.
The Co-op has SODA SUPPLIES, PRODUCE and KITCHEN + BOOKS shelf categories.
Finish a purchase or return unbought goods before changing categories.
At home, click BACK >, then RECIPES above the compact table. Put an owned
book, ingredients and required water/cell in INPUT; choose a recipe and CRAFT.
The saved worktop uses native recipe details, item previews and inventories.
Full OUTPUT refuses crafting before consuming any ingredients or resources.
The five books contain all 240 recipes from Megablock Kitchen Recipe Book v2.
Kitchen requirements accumulate as follows:
  1. Counter Kitchen: back-room counter at home — 33 recipes.
  2. Pantry Kitchen: also an owned crate in rack slot 1 — 31 recipes.
  3. Water Kitchen: also fitted Waterworks — 30 recipes.
  4. Hotplate Kitchen: also a fitted Dockside Galley — 68 recipes.
  5. Cold Store Kitchen: also a fitted fridge — 78 recipes.
Buy and fit equipment through Joe Wild's BACK ROOM tab.

An explicit Water ingredient consumes 250 mL of 99.5%+ clean water from your
owned container. Wet stove dishes without that ingredient use 100 mL cooking
water. Stove recipes use 2 cell power; fridge recipes use 1. Every recipe
lists the exact cost. Water containers, cells, books and fitted stations stay.
Dry mixes, doughs, batters, marinades and other intermediate preparations are
ingredients for later recipes. Finished foods use native eating and trading.
Moldy or unowned produce cannot be used. New food values remain provisional
until a sustained live economy playtest.

FRUIT AND EXISTING SODAS
All 20 existing brands, saved item identifiers and can artwork are preserved.
The Fizzworks Recipe Book provides both concentrate and fresh-fruit recipes.
Fresh fruit makes four 350 mL or four 500 mL cans. Each batch uses one fruit,
one sweetener, four fizz charges, four matching empty cans, 1400/2000 mL water
and 8/12 cell power. Classic blends also use the named botanical:
  Nutrifruit + cola spices -> Quay Cola
  Pear + dried ginger -> Ginger Brass
  Lychee + vanilla pod -> Vanilla Velvet
  Date + root spices -> Rootline Root Beer
The other sixteen fruits match their named brand. Each filled can keeps its
own empty can's wear, dents and rust, plus the source water's quality.
Soda water must be at least 35% pure. Clean water gives better results.

Engineering sells Galley and Workshop recipe books. The Galley book covers
the twelve existing meals and snacks; Workshop covers concentrates, flour,
sweetener and empty packaging. Both require an owned Dockside Galley.
The Fizzworks book requires an owned canner. Machines retain their production
panels and automatic overnight production. The old production handbook still
works and covers all Galley recipes; old bottling handbooks keep their recipes.

BACK ROOM AND OTHER CONTENT
BACK > on the shop's right wall opens the back room. The compact original
table and layout are restored to fit the grid and room. The item-scale fix
and Joe's native fitting/dismantling controls remain. The counter inventory
is shared with the front shop. Installed equipment and cargo keep their IDs.
Use the after-closing map for the four shops. Use the content guide to find
new factions; new customers arrive through ordinary random visits.

CAPABILITY NOTICE
The game requests "Load or generate code at runtime" because Neon Undertow
uses Harmony runtime patches for game behavior, including starting-trait
limits and the faction reputation display. The native scanner detects the
dynamic-code capability. This is a real code-trust permission, not an art
permission or a requirement to download additional code. Enable trusted mods.

VALIDATION
All 240 kitchen recipes and 117 production-book recipes were exercised in
the real game with native previews, material consumption, water/power costs
and separate can conditions. Native counter save reconstruction and full-
output conservation passed. Recipe chooser, crafting buttons, restored small
table and categorized shop shelves were checked in game. Long-term economy
balance and arbitrary older mod combinations are not comprehensively tested.
No save files, QA helpers or third-party mod files are included. Original
dependencies are downloaded separately. See CREDITS.txt and DEPENDENCIES.json.
