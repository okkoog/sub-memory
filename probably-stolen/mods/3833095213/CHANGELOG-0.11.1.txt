NEON UNDERTOW: NPC TOTAL OVERHAUL + MORE — 0.11.1

START HERE
Enable Neon Undertow, original Brewing Expansion (3829730904), and original
Tobacco Expansion (3832840208) in Mods, then restart Probably Stolen.
No activation key is needed. Look for NEON UNDERTOW · ACTIVE at the top left.
Click it for the content guide, installed version and dependency status.

WHERE TO FIND THE CONTENT
- Load or start a run. Guide > Find content > Show new factions jumps past the
  vanilla groups. Previous / Next displays all enabled faction pages.
- Open your shop: new customers arrive through ordinary random visits.
  Story arrivals are unchanged; not every visitor will be a new character.
- After closing, use the travel/map menu: Quayside Canning Co-op sells
  ingredients, Stormglass Depot Outlet sells machinery and storage, and
  Glassmarket Counter & Fixture Stall sells counter decorations. Undertow
  Engineering sells production modules, control racks and bottle magazines.
- BACK > on the right wall opens the back room. It shares your front counter
  stock. Use Joe Wild's BACK ROOM tab for bay expansions, storage racks and
  per-bay fitting and dismantling directly in that tab. Buy equipment before fitting it; later days and credits
  unlock more bays. One station per bay. Dismantling costs credits. Click the
  entire back-room door to return, or click installed equipment to use it.
- Inspect new fruit and vegetables with the same interface as vanilla grapes.
  Existing native condition states are used; this is not a new timed-rot system.

This hotfix moves fitting and dismantling fully into Joe Wild, preserves item
scale across repeated moves, restores four supplier shops, and corrects the
back-room table perspective. Equipment and cargo retain their identity.

The expansion includes 20 vegetables (six inspection appearances each), the local
fruit inspection expansion, the back-room system, and the getting-started UI.
Medical, gun, ammo and other planned room paths remain future content.
The soda fruit/fizz recipe redesign and complete can-label redraw are still
in development; current soda production continues to use concentrates.

Original dependencies are downloaded separately; no third-party mod code or
assets are included. See CREDITS.txt and DEPENDENCIES.json.
