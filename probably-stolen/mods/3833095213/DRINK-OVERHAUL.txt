NEON UNDERTOW 0.10.0 — FIRST SODA OVERHAUL BATCH

20 REGULAR BRANDS, 100 CONDITION APPEARANCES
Following your clarification, the first 100 icons are 20 brands x five states:
pristine, worn, dented, rusty and badly damaged. They are not 100 separate flavors.
The 10 x 10 sheet shows two brands per row, five states of each from left to right.
All regular cans use exactly the same native 16 x 32 canvas, silhouette, lid and
baseline. Damage is represented by surface wear, corrosion and creases; it does
not change the inventory footprint. The regular can remains one by two cells.

The existing 10 regular brands keep their IDs. Ten regular-size versions of the
existing large-can brands complete the 20. The existing ten 500 mL cans remain
available with their original IDs and larger native silhouette. They also share
the condition/inspection rules, but the full new large-can range is a later stage.

HOW TO USE
Buy supplies at Quayside Canning Co-op after closing. New blank cans are sound.
The Reclaimed Can Case contains 20 regular and 10 large cans with mixed damage.
Move your selected cans, concentrate and a water container into canner INPUT.
Use a magnifier to inspect a can or finished soda through the normal inspection
action. Empty cans also open inspection through Activate. The canner has INSPECT
INPUT and INSPECT OUTPUT buttons for owned items in those compartments.
CANS: best inspected first prioritizes inspected packaging by condition. It does
not silently reveal the condition of uninspected cans. Default is input order.
Use Category to switch between regular and large-can recipes.

The canner takes one matching empty can, one matching flavor concentrate, 350 mL
water and two power per regular can (500 mL / three power for existing large cans).
Water must contain at least 35% water: the lowest named native water grade. Below
that, the game calls it unknown water and the canner refuses it. Galley food and
concentrate recipes retain their earlier 99.5% minimum. Existing bottle machinery
is unchanged. Make one or use overnight AUTO; available power, supplies and output
space still limit production. Empty cans at zero durability are unusable.

QUALITY MAPPING — VERIFIED AGAINST THE INSTALLED GAME
Scale: integer 0..10000, where 10000 is 100.00% source-water purity.
Source band                       Minimum    Native innate value modifier
Pure Water                         9990                    +100%
High Quality Water                 9900                     +50%
Basewater                          9800                       0%
Ghostwater                         9600                     -33%
Rustwater                          9000                     -75%
Gutterflow                         3500                     -90%

Container penalty uses the native integer durability valuation formula:
 -(100 - current*100/maximum)/2 - (100 - maximum*100/original)/2
Add the verified native dent penalty (-5) and rust penalty (-40), as applicable.
Clamp the total to -100..0. Soda quality score = source purity * (100+penalty)/100,
rounded down. This combination is a NEW soda rule; it is not native water physics.
The score is a condition-adjusted quality measure, not measured chemical purity.
Apply the native band modifier for that final score once, through an innate item
feature. The generic durability modifier is neutralized on these items to avoid
charging the same damage twice. Market, faction and negotiation effects still
apply normally. Below-Gutterflow scores retain the lowest native value tier.

Example: 10000 source, intact can => score 10000, Pure grade, +100% innate value.
The same water with a dent but full durability => score 9500, Rust grade, -75%.
These native water bands make the initial damage curve deliberately steep;
further balancing can change the soda retention rule without changing brands.

Freshness/quality does not recolor an opaque metal can's brand. The native water
system mixes liquid colors from its actual liquid contents; it does not assign
one fixed paint color per grade. We preserve that actual source color and show
its swatch in inspection. Labels identify brands; grade labels describe the item.

PERSISTENCE AND INSPECTION
Saved item tags keep schema, source purity, source color, origin, filling-time
container durability, current durability, dents/rust, inspection flag and final
quality separate. Later damage recomputes final score without overwriting the
original source-water snapshot. Moving/storing items does not change it.
Inspection only marks information as examined. It never cleans or repairs cans,
alters source water, raises a grade, consumes supplies or adds value.
Existing saved sodas with no recorded source are explicitly unrecorded. Their
source grade is not invented. New supplier offers declare 99.00% producer water.

WHAT IS STILL NEXT
The new large-can range, actual premium glass-bottle items/recipes/inspection,
alcohol and further drinks are not included in this first regular-can batch.
A separate glass-packaging price helper is tested (1.6x base packaging category)
and the data model reserves a premium flag; this is groundwork, not a shipped
glass-soda catalogue. Glass grade will still depend on water and condition.
No new spoilage/health effects, cleaning recipes or repair recipes are introduced.
