农业扩展1.7.5 / Agri Expansion 1.7.5

【集成农场图文手册】
新增投入→操作→取出的像素流程插图，14章均增加三步图解和实际页面操作路径。详细规则另页阅读，涵盖修复、集成、种植、轮作、水电、仓储、养殖、矿物质与埃诺莉订单。中英文同步，保留章节与详细说明阅读位置；已有手册直接更新，无需重新领取。

【重型农场主机保护】
官方模组设置新增“重型农场主机保护”，默认开启、无需重启。主机太大太重，夜间小偷无法搬走；主机及内部物品不作为夜间盗窃目标，也不计入保险估值。取到主机外的货物照常处理。买卖价格、保险折扣和难度倍率保持原样；关闭设置后恢复原版判定。

无需新增权限，保留现有农业、料理、香水和剧情功能。请完全退出并重启游戏以载入新版DLL。

【Illustrated farm handbook】
Added a pixel-art Intake → Action → Export flow, fourteen three-step illustrated guides and page-by-page operation paths. Detailed rules have their own pages, covering repairs, integration, crops, queues, water, power, storage, livestock, nutrients and Eleanor orders. Chinese and English are supported; chapter/detail bookmarks are preserved. Existing handbooks update automatically.

【Heavy mainframe protection】
Added an official mod setting, ON by default, with no restart required when toggled. The farm mainframe is too large and heavy to carry off: it and its contents are excluded from night burglary and insurance valuation. Withdrawn goods follow normal rules. Trading prices, insurance discounts and difficulty multipliers are unchanged. Turn it off to restore native rules.

No new permissions. Existing crops, cooking, perfumery and story content are retained. Fully exit and restart the game to load the updated DLL.
