# 农场使用手册

游戏内独立手册正文，中英文随游戏语言切换。

## 1. 开机以前 / Before switching on

这台机子不是买回来通上电就能长菜。先翻开终端，看看哪几根线路还断着。

平时往里送东西，用“投入”；里面的收成和退回的东西，到“取出”里拿。投入栏不是仓库，东西搁在那里，并不算已经交给农场了。

尤其是整台设备：先把里面的货、液体和外加模组取干净，再接进农场。接上就算农场的一部分，别指望隔天还能拎出来卖。

我把各处的用法分开记了。第一次用，照目录从前往后看；哪一处出毛病，再翻回那一页。

This rig will not grow a thing just because you plug it in. Open the terminal and check the broken circuits first.

Intake is where you put things you want to hand over. Export holds harvests and returned goods. Leaving something in Intake does not mean it has been stored or installed. Press the proper deposit or integration control.

Before integrating a whole machine, empty its goods, liquid and extra modules. Once connected, it stays part of the farm. You cannot pull it out tomorrow and sell it.

First time here? Read these notes in order. After that, turn to whichever bit is giving you trouble.

## 2. 把终端修起来 / Repairing the terminal

修机别一口气把家底全倒进去。终端上列着这一档缺什么，备齐哪一种，就放进投入栏，按“提交修复材料”。分几趟送也认账，用不上的会给你留下。

头一档要电子零件3、铜线3、废金属6、打印材料3。修好后，有10升空水箱、100电的储电位置和100件物资的仓位；种子也有一页地方。水和电得自己添，我没留满罐。

往后两档，四种材料各要6、12份；再下一档各24份。这里用的是金属锭，不再收废金属。

最后一档换成超科技电子、管制化学品、最高品质金属锭和仓储拓展模块，各4份。别拿普通锭往里塞。

Bring repairs in batches. The terminal lists what the next repair needs. Put it in Intake and press Contribute repair materials. It keeps a tally and leaves unsuitable goods alone.

First repair: 3 electronics, 3 wire, 6 scrap metal and 3 printing material. That gives you an empty 10L reservoir, room for 100 energy, 100 goods and one seed page. Supply your own water and charge.

The next repairs need 6, then 12, then 24 of each ordinary material. Use ingots rather than scrap after the first repair.

The final repair needs 4 each: hypertech electronics, controlled chemicals, perfect ingots and storage expansion kits. Ordinary ingots will not do.

## 3. 接设备与扩容 / Connecting equipment

主机修起第一档，就能往各处接设备了。把清空的设备放进投入栏，到总览按“集成投入栏的设备”。没接上的东西还在原处，先看提示，别再买一台来试。

一台水培系统开一块种植区；鼠笼和循环水养殖系统开养殖区。集水器、净水器和水箱归水路，充能器、能量阵列和电池归电路，空储藏区与拓展模块添仓位。

各处随接入的设备升档，终端另修。终端从第一到第五档，能同时照管4、8、12、18、24块种植区。

水箱、电池添的容量另外算。大仓和已经扩过的笼子，原有地方也给你算进去，不必先拆回小的。

After the first terminal repair, put empty equipment in Intake and integrate it from Overview. Rejected equipment stays there. Read the reason before buying another.

Hydroponics add plots. Cages and recirculating aquaculture systems add animal areas. Collectors, purifiers and tanks go to water; rechargers, power arrays and batteries to power; empty storage bays and expansion kits add storage.

Those sections improve with connected equipment. Repair the terminal separately. Its five levels support 4, 8, 12, 18 and 24 active plots.

Donated tanks and batteries add their capacity. Large storage bays and expanded cages keep their extra space.

## 4. 先种第一茬 / Your first crop

第一茬先挑好养的，别上来就种一排娇贵货。接一台空水培系统，添好水、电，再到种植页打开第一块区。

把一颗种子放进本区种子位，看看培养桶有没有水。新接进来的培养桶先补到一成，够用再调；需要时开本区自动供水，再确认本区显示“已开启”。图里的植株就是眼前这一茬，旁边有长势、健康和停机缘由。

收成会进物资仓，种子回种子库。默认记住这一茬，收完还会找同种的种子接着种；所以库里也备几颗。

多年生的想换掉，到本区“更多设置”里选“砍伐植株”。这会毁掉当前植株并关掉本区，不用另找斧头。想重种，选好种子再把本区打开。

Use an easy crop for the first batch. Connect an empty hydroponic system, supply water and charge, then open the first plot from Growing.

Put a seed in its seed slot. Check the culture bucket. New plots start with a 10% refill target; adjust it when needed. Enable automatic water supply if needed, and check the plot itself is ON. The plant picture, progress and health describe this crop.

Harvest goes to goods storage, seeds to the seed bank. Repeat mode remembers the crop and looks for another matching seed after harvest, so leave some in reserve.

For a perennial you want gone, choose Cut down plant in More settings. It destroys that plant and switches the plot off; no separate axe is needed. Select a new seed and turn the plot back on to restart.

## 5. 种子与轮作 / Seeds and planting order

好种子别和多余的混着扔。先存进种子库，把想留下的保护好；摆放、性状和大小都照原样留着。

同种续种，适合长供货的庄稼。要轮作，终端修到第二档，把本区模式改成“队列”，再把种子放进那一排。它按顺序找，队列里没种子就等，不会替你乱换。

第三档起，播种时有一成机会帮你优选性状；第四档是三成，还能选优选方向。不是每颗都改，别拿最宝贝的种子赌这个。

第四档也能帮着调培养液，但矿物质片要先存好。本区供水、补水比例、矿物质比例和优化都能分开调；手动照料的那块，关掉不想让它代办的事。

Keep prized seeds apart from surplus. Put them in the seed bank and protect the ones you want to keep. Their traits, size and placement stay with them.

Repeat planting suits a steady supply crop. At terminal II, choose Queue for a plot and put seeds in its queue. It follows that order and waits when none are left.

Terminal III has a 10% chance to improve a seed when planting; IV has 30% and lets you choose the direction. It is a chance, not a promise.

At IV it can also manage nutrients. Store nutrient tablets first. Each plot has separate water, fill-ratio, mineral-ratio and optimization settings. Switch off whatever you prefer to tend yourself.

## 6. 水从哪来 / Keeping water moving

装水容器放进投入栏，到水资源页按导入。水留下，容器拿回去。各档水分开存，水库满了就不再收。

集水器添水，净水器除杂，水箱添容量。三只空水箱，可不会自己装满。

待净化水质是处理哪路水；目标水质是净到哪档；优先使用水质是先送哪档。种植区“更多设置”能单独选，芦苇就留给污水；没单独选的跟着总设置走。

每次给种植区补水只取一档，首选不够用时先找一档够用的，不拿几档凑满。桶里旧液不会自动倒掉，想换水先回存。新接的盆先补一成，需要更多再调。

要取水，往投入栏放空容器或有余量的同水质容器，再按提取。设备干了什么，翻水处理明细看。

Import filled vessels from Water. You keep the vessel. Grades stay separate; a full reservoir stops accepting more.

Collectors make water, purifiers remove impurities, tanks add space. Empty tanks will not fill themselves.

Source chooses what to purify, Target how far, Use first what to supply. In a plot's More settings, choose another grade or follow the global choice. Give reeds dirty water.

Each plot refill draws one grade. If the first cannot meet its cycle need, another sufficient grade is chosen. Existing culture stays; return it before changing water. New plots start at 10%; adjust as needed.

To withdraw water, put empty or matching-grade vessels in Intake. Check the detailed record for actual work.

## 7. 电别全花光 / Keeping a charge

拿电池给农场充电，和把电池拆进农场，不是一回事。

只想转电量，就把有电的电池放在投入栏，按“从电池转移电量”。电池留给你。想永久添储电位置，才去集成设备；电池这部分最多添一万。

充能器每天10电，能量阵列15电，增殖器4电。有了商用供电，前两样翻倍，增殖器照旧。电浆草叶也能放进投入栏给农场充电。

想给外面的电池充，放入能充电且没满的电池，再按“提取电力到电池”。

睡前看一眼发电与耗电明细。发电数是进账，主机、水路、种植和养殖都会花电；储电满了，多出来的不会替你攒到明晚。

Charging from a battery is different from permanently installing it.

For a transfer, put a charged battery in Intake and choose Transfer battery energy. You keep it. Integrate a battery only to add permanent capacity; that part is limited to 10,000.

Rechargers generate 10 per day, power arrays 15, breeders 4. Commercial power doubles the first two, not breeders. Plasma leaves in Intake can also charge the farm.

For an outside rechargeable battery, put it in Intake with spare capacity and withdraw energy into it.

Check the power record before bed. The mainframe, water equipment, crops and animals all spend energy. Anything generated beyond storage capacity is lost.

## 8. 仓库里的东西 / Getting goods back

刚修好的仓位不大，先别把整柜菜都搬来。作物、养殖产物和粪便都能存，种子另进种子库；不是所有杂货都收。

把东西放在投入栏，再按存入。留在栏里的，就是没收下，别当它已经进仓。

取货时打开物资仓，先选东西，再选取出1、10、100件或全部。货会放到主机的取出栏，要从那里拿走。取出栏满了，剩下的还在仓里，不会丢。

仓位按件数记，不是看图占几格。品质照旧，食物也照常变质，这里不是冷库。

新种类、新规格的收成，先不拿去喂牲畜。进物资仓选中它，手动准许喂食才会用；大小分开记，同规格再入仓还照你的选择办。饲料保留数量也能另设。

Start small. Goods storage accepts crops, animal products and manure, not every piece of shop junk. Seeds use the seed bank.

Put goods in Intake and deposit them. Anything left there was not accepted.

To withdraw, open goods storage, select an item, then 1, 10, 100 or all. It goes to the mainframe Export tray. Take it from there. If Export is full, the rest stays in storage.

Capacity counts units, not occupied cells. Quality is retained, but food still spoils. This is no cold room.

New food types and sizes are not used automatically. Select one in storage and allow it for feeding. Sizes are remembered separately; later stock of the same size follows your choice. You can set a separate feed reserve too.

## 9. 笼里的活物 / Looking after livestock

先把鼠笼里的活物和外加设备取出来，再接笼子。接好后打开养殖区，把动物放进去。每区的喂食器归农场，拿不走，但能照常打开。

把食物拖进喂食器，按旁边“启动”，才是搅成饲料卡路里。它不会当场把一笼鼠喂饱；夜里按设定的饱腹度吃。开了自动混合，新食物也会自己搅。

自动喂食开着时，农场会从准许使用的库存里补饲料，留下你设的保留量。倭瓜和倭瓜做的饲料能加快生长，管三天，反复吃是续时间，不是越吃越快。

捐一台自动屠宰机后，各区才有屠宰机。能直接拖动物过去，也能打开改条件；留种、标记和起过名的，先检查保护设置。

Empty the cage of creatures and extra equipment before integration. Then open its animal area and put livestock in. Its feeder belongs to the farm: usable, but not removable.

Drop food into the feeder and press Start to grind it into stored calories. That does not instantly fill every stomach. Animals feed at night to the chosen fullness. Auto-mixing also grinds newly inserted food.

Automatic feeding draws allowed food from storage while keeping your reserve. Gourds and gourd feed speed growth for three days. More refreshes the time, not the multiplier.

Donate an auto butcher to unlock one in each area. Drop animals onto it or open its conditions. Check protections for breeding stock, marked animals and named pets.

## 10. 水里养的那些 / Fish need more than water

循环水养殖系统接进来以前，把鱼、鱼卵和外加设备拿出来。原配绑着的水箱不用硬拆，里面剩的水也留着。过滤件先取下，接好再装回。

养殖页里，鱼池在一边，水箱、过滤器和两台机器在旁边。看见水库有水，不代表鱼池就一定够；还要看本区供水和实际水量。

鱼不是全都吃一样的。肉食鱼要肉，工业饲料和污泥饲料不是肉的替身；也别拿这两样去喂鼠。

没适口饲料的鱼会停下来，别的鱼和鱼卵未必一起停。先看本区提示，再看喂食器分类储量。

要添地方，把金属锭放进主机投入栏，再扩展本区。一锭添10格，单区最多96格，全场最多六区。

Before integrating a recirculating system, remove fish, roe and extra equipment. Leave its attached original tank in place; its remaining liquid stays. Remove filter pieces first and reinstall them afterwards.

The fish grid, tank, filters and permanent machines are shown together. Water in the reservoir does not guarantee enough in this tank. Check actual supply.

Diets matter. Carnivorous fish need meat; industrial feed and sludge pellets are not substitutes. Those feeds are not for rats either.

Fish lacking suitable feed pause; other fish and roe may still continue. Read the area status and feeder category amounts.

For more room, put an ingot in mainframe Intake and expand the area: 10 cells per ingot, 96 per area, six areas total.

## 11. 专用模组怎么用 / Special crop equipment

专门照料某种作物的模组，先从原机器上取下来，再交给主机。每种交一件就够了，重复那件会留在投入栏。

想整场一起用，到“全场种植与模组设置”，选那一种模组，再选全部开启。只想关某一种，也在这里办，不必挨个区去找。

一块区不适合，就到它的“更多设置”改本区专用效果。别的区照旧。以后添的新种植区，照最近一次全场安排来。

再做一次全场安排，会把旧区也一起改；只翻页面，不会偷偷改回去。见到“部分开启”，就是各区安排不一样，不是没装好。

有的作物几种专用效果碰上，还是按原来的先后算。不是多交一件，就能让同一种作用叠个没完。

Remove crop-specific modules from other machines before donating them. One of each kind unlocks that effect; duplicates stay in Intake.

For the whole farm, open All plots / special modules, select a module and enable all. Disable a single module there too.

For an exception, change that plot under More settings / Crop-specific effects. Other plots keep their arrangement. New plots follow the last whole-farm default.

Another whole-farm command changes existing plots together. Simply opening a page does not undo individual choices. Mixed means the areas have different settings, not a broken installation.

Original priorities still apply when crop effects meet. Donating duplicates does not stack the same effect endlessly.

## 12. 回收与培养液 / Waste and nutrients

种子多到没地方放，再回收，别刚收下一把就全碾了。终端第二档起，6颗多余种子换1片矿物质片；保护着的、正在种的、排进队的，不拿去碾。

先设好每种要留多少。自动回收开着，往后多出来的才按这个数处理。动物粪便也能压，10件换1片，在回收设置里办。

矿物质片放投入栏后存入，才算进主机的肥料余量。要单区补，就打开那块种植区，按补充矿物质片。

补水比例管桶装到几成；矿物质比例管培养液浓淡。别把这两个认成一回事。想把多余培养液收回来，用回存水库那一项；水库装不下就先留在桶里。

Recycle surplus seeds, not your last good handful. Terminal II can turn six surplus seeds into one nutrient tablet. Protected, planted and queued seeds are excluded.

Set the reserve for each kind first. Automatic recycling works on the surplus above that number. Manure can also be compressed at ten units per tablet from Recycling settings.

Deposit tablets from Intake to add them to the mainframe reserve. For a single plot, open it and use the nutrient-tablet control.

Fill percentage sets how full the bucket should be. Mineral ratio sets nutrient concentration. They are different. Use Return excess culture to send extra liquid back; if the reservoir cannot fit it, it stays in the bucket.

## 13. 不干活时先查什么 / When it stops working

看见没长，先查水、电、饲料和仓位，别上来就拆机子。

先看总览有没有缺东西；再到对应那一页。种植区写缺水，就查培养桶和本区自动供水；写缺电，就查实际储电，别只看明晚打算发多少。

水处理明细里，集水是添了多少水，除杂是滤掉多少脏东西，两者不是同一个数。电力明细里，各处扣的电加起来才是这一晚的花销。

仓满，采收和屠宰会停。把物资从仓里取到取出栏，还得把取出栏搬空。

种植区自己关着，也不会长；有些是砍伐后停下的。调好再开。主页面关了，旁边开着的工作页不会跟着关，同一个入口再按一次能收起来。

If nothing grows, check water, charge, feed and storage before dismantling anything.

Start with Overview, then the affected area. No water? Check the bucket and that plot's automatic supply. No energy? Check what is stored, not what you hope to generate tomorrow.

Collected water is not the same figure as removed impurities. In the power record, category deductions add up to that night's spending.

Full storage pauses harvest and slaughter. Withdrawing to Export is not enough: clear Export too.

A switched-off plot will not grow. Cutting down a plant leaves it off until you restart it. Closing the dashboard leaves work windows open; press the same entry again to close one.

## 14. 跟埃诺莉交货 / Keeping your deliveries straight

要是这台机子是埃诺莉家交给你修的，她的账另记，别和卖给路人的零散生意混了。

她来时看看收购和订单。答应哪一单，先记清作物、数量和期限，再占种植区；别把所有地方押在一茬还没长成的东西上。

总览能打开她留下的合同。按单子把要交的东西放进主机投入栏，再去交付；东西还在仓库里，她不会替你翻。该领的报酬也记得领。

暂时交不上，就早些看看还差多少。别以为把种子买回来，就等于已经有收成。

有的农场主机是另外买来的，没有这份供货约定，照前面几页管好自家农场就行。

If Eleanor's family entrusted you with this rig, keep her account separate from ordinary passing trade.

When she visits, inspect her buying list and orders. Check the crop, amount and deadline before promising a plot. Do not stake every area on one unfinished batch.

Open her contract from Overview. Put requested contributions in mainframe Intake and submit them there; she will not search your warehouse for you. Remember to claim earned rewards.

Check shortages early. Buying seeds is not the same as having a harvest.

If you bought a mainframe separately, that supply agreement does not come with it. The rest of this notebook still applies.
